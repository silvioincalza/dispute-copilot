Mastercard Chargeback Rules
Mastercard reason code 4853 applies to cardholder disputes involving goods or services not as described. Evidence should summarize the cardholder complaint and merchant response.
Mastercard reason code 4863 applies to cardholder disputes for cardholder does not recognize potential fraud. Investigators should review authentication, merchant location, device fingerprint, and prior spending patterns.
When strong customer authentication was successfully completed, analysts must document the 3DS version, challenge outcome, and any exemption claims before recommending a write-off.
