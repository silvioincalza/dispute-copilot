SCA and 3DS Review Policy
When a transaction is disputed, analysts must review SCA status and 3DS version before drafting a chargeback. AUTHENTICATED transactions with 3DS 2.x may shift liability depending on the scheme reason code and merchant category.
NOT_AUTHENTICATED transactions require additional fraud indicators, issuer notes, and device telemetry review.
EXEMPTED transactions require the exemption rationale, transaction risk score, and merchant acquirer evidence.
Every agent response must cite the specific policy file used for recommendations.
